How it helps your device and your use of the platform

Regular scanning builds a history of your risk score over time, which is how the Timeline and Risk Trend views help you notice when something changes. Automatic and on-demand wide scans, where your plan includes them, look for weak points across your account and device settings so that problems are spotted early rather than after something goes wrong. Priority analysis shortens the wait for results, and detailed reports tell you exactly what to fix and why.

Staying subscribed also means fewer interruptions. You won't be stopped mid-task by the free scan limit, and the safety checks you rely on keep running in the background without any extra effort from you.

What happens when you unsubscribe

  • Your plan ends and the paid features above stop working.
  • Your account returns to the free tier, which allows 2 scans in total. Scans you have already run still count toward that limit, so you may be asked to subscribe again before you can scan.
  • Your past scan results, timeline and settings stay in your account and remain available to view.
  • You won't be charged again for this subscription.
  • Scheduled and automatic scans stop, and you'll no longer receive their alerts.

Coming back later

You can resubscribe at any time from the Subscribe page and choose the same plan or a different one. Your history will still be here when you do.
